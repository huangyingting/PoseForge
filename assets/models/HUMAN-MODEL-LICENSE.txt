Primary realistic body models
=============================

Generated with MakeHuman Community 1.3.0:
https://www.makehumancommunity.org/

The MakeHuman core assets used to generate these models are released under
Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

The generated female and male bodies were created specifically for this
project with distinct gender, height, muscle, weight, and body-proportion
parameters, the MakeHuman Asian macro target, and the CC0 young Asian skin
materials. They use MakeHuman's game-engine skeleton and were exported to
glTF/GLB through Blender.
