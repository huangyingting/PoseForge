Primary realistic body models
=============================

Generated with MakeHuman Community 1.3.0:
https://www.makehumancommunity.org/

The MakeHuman core assets used to generate these models - the base mesh, the
macro and breast targets, the game-engine skeleton and the skin materials -
are released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal:
https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.ASSETS.md

The twelve bodies were created specifically for this project: female, male and
neutral (MakeHuman gender 0.5), each with its own height, muscle, weight and
body-proportion parameters, in four models that change only the ethnic or age
macro target and the skin:

  realistic-<type>.glb           Asian macro, age about 22     skin-<type>.png            young Asian skin
  realistic-<type>-european.glb  Caucasian macro, about 22     skin-<type>-european.png   young Caucasian skin
  realistic-<type>-african.glb   African macro, about 22       skin-<type>-african.png    young African skin
  realistic-<type>-mature.glb    Asian macro, about 50         skin-<type>-mature.png     middle-aged Asian skin

where <type> is female, male or neutral. The neutral bodies wear the female
skins, so there is no skin-neutral*.png. Some bodies also carry MakeHuman
breast-position modifiers. Every skin-*.png is the unmodified 2048px diffuse
photograph of the named CC0 MakeHuman skin. The meshes were exported through
Blender to glTF/GLB.

They are regenerated by

  node scripts/models/make-bodies.mjs

from the parameters in scripts/models/bodies.json. (realistic-female.glb and
realistic-male.glb are the project's original exports, kept as they were; the
script reproduces their vertices exactly.)

Hair, eyebrows and eyelashes (hair/)
====================================

MakeHuman Community 1.3.0's own proxies, also CC0 1.0 Universal under the same
asset licence: the hairstyles afro01, bob01, bob02, braid01, long01,
ponytail01 and short01-short04, the eyebrows eyebrow009 and eyebrow010 and the
eyelashes eyelashes01 and eyelashes02. MakeHuman fitted each of them to each of
the twelve bodies above (cards-<body>.bin, with cards.bin holding what the
bodies share); the textures (<name>.png) are MakeHuman's diffuse textures
reduced to grey and alpha, halved to 1024px for the hairstyles, so the app can
tint them. They are regenerated by

  node scripts/models/make-hair.mjs

which runs MakeHuman through scripts/models/makehuman_body.py and converts the
textures with scripts/models/card_textures.py.
